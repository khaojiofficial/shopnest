Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and express takes 1 to 2. Orders over $100 ship free with standard delivery.
What is your return policy?
Return unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or an exchange.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit and debit cards, and cash on delivery in selected areas. Card payments are encrypted.
How do I use a discount code?
Enter the code in the discount box on the cart or checkout page and select Apply. Your savings appear in the order summary.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Yes, until it ships. Contact support with your order number and we'll update it.
Do products come with a warranty?
Most electronics include a 1 to 2 year manufacturer warranty. The details are listed under Specifications on each product page.
